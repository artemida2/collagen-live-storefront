## Users

Adults in Russia, mostly women 30+, buying an edible collagen course for themselves: skin, hair,
nails, joints. They arrive already half-convinced that collagen is worth taking and are choosing
*which* collagen — so the decision they are actually making is powder versus jelly, and this brand
versus a cheaper capsule. A second, smaller audience is people buying for joints and sport
recovery. They land on a laptop, compare against the manufacturer's own site, and want to know the
price, the flavours, and how fast it ships to their city.

## Product Purpose

Sell Collagen Live Wellness — an edible "live" collagen in jelly form — as an authorised
distributor, and make the jelly-versus-powder difference legible in the first minute. Success is a
placed order for a 0.5 kg jar or a set.

## Positioning

The distributor sells the manufacturer's product, so the positioning is the manufacturer's, taken
verbatim from collagen-live.ru: Collagen Live is produced by the **Multiferment DDL** technology,
the gentlest known extraction, in which the collagen molecule is not destroyed and its structure is
not broken. The result is a jelly rather than a powder, and the manufacturer therefore calls it
*live* — retaining the maximum of its natural properties, and absorbed at **96 %**.

The contrast the manufacturer itself draws, and which no competing powder can copy: to make powdered
collagen the raw material is dried below 40 °C, then **heated to 120 °C** and only then ground; that
high temperature alters the collagen molecules and they lose part of their natural properties.

Because there are no preservatives, the product must be kept refrigerated at **+2…+6 °C**, like any
fresh food. This is a constraint, and it is also proof of the claim.

## Operating Context

Bought as a course, not as a one-off: a 0.5 kg jar is a **1.5-month** course, 1 kg is a **3-month**
course. Eaten by the spoon straight from the jar or dissolved in water, as part of a daily morning
routine, and stored in the fridge door. Ordering happens on a laptop; delivery is by courier or
pickup, and the order is confirmed by a manager on the phone rather than by online payment.

## Product Principles

1. The jelly-versus-powder difference is the whole argument; every section either proves it or gets
   out of its way.
2. Claims are the manufacturer's and are quoted as such — nothing about efficacy is invented or
   sharpened here.
3. The refrigeration requirement is presented as evidence of a live product, not hidden as a
   drawback.
4. It is sold as a course, not a jar: the visitor should understand 0.5 kg = 1.5 months before they
   see the price.
5. The distributor is national by presentation and Crimean by logistics; the warehouse is stated
   once, factually, and never used as the headline.
